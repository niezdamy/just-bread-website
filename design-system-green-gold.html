Reference · 03

Design language · evergreen with a gold accent

A little more editorial. Still unmistakably bread.

A considered extension for the FAQ, contact, and analytics consent touchpoint. Built on a deep evergreen with brand gold #FFB300 as the bright accent — gold marks the moments that matter (hero highlights, calls to action, icons) while evergreen carries structure and text; a restrained serif display face adds editorial character while Poppins keeps longer copy familiar and easy to scan.

01 / Palette

Evergreen, flour, and gold

Evergreen anchors heroes, text, and structure. Brand gold is the accent: highlights, primary buttons, and hover states. Warm paper keeps large areas calm.

✳#FFB300 / #FBF9F41.7 : 1FailsDecorative only — never text, icons that carry meaning, or focus
  • Gold never on lightGold text or thin icons on paper or white fall to 1.6–1.7:1. Use gold only on evergreen, or as a filled shape holding evergreen text.
  • Fixed in this reviewCard numbers and the FAQ numerals were #889182 (3.1:1); they now use forest grey (5.8:1). The gold FAQ sparks became moss and are hidden from screen readers.
  • Minimum 12px, 44px targetsLabels, helper text, and buttons were 9–11px; all are now at least 12px. Buttons and the CTA are at least 44px tall.
  • Visible focus3px moss outline on light surfaces, gold on evergreen, always offset by 3px. Hover colour is never the only state cue.
  • Not colour aloneLinks stay underlined; consent choices differ by label, not by colour. Equal-weight outlined buttons avoid a pre-selected look.
  • Motion and languageAnimations respect prefers-reduced-motion. Pages declare lang (en / pl) so screen readers pick the right voice.
